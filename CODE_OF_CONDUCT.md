# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, kindness, respect for different opinions and experiences, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, apologizing for mistakes, and prioritizing the community's well-being.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces, including through an official account or as an appointed representative.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately through the repository's GitHub **Report abuse** action or through a maintainer's publicly listed private contact method. Do not post private incident details in public issues. If no private maintainer contact is listed, use GitHub's reporting process. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly and respect the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction:** For inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior, maintainers may issue a private warning explaining the violation and requesting correction or an apology.
2. **Warning:** For a violation or repeated behavior, maintainers may issue a warning with consequences and restrict interaction for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban:** For a serious or sustained violation, maintainers may temporarily prohibit participation and public or private interaction with affected community members.
4. **Permanent ban:** For a pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of groups, maintainers may permanently prohibit participation.

Consequences depend on severity, impact, and prior behavior. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
